arrecIA privacy policy

arrecIA is a personal assistant with a single user, its owner. No other person uses it.

  • What it accesses: Google Calendar events, read-only (scope calendar.readonly), of calendars the owner shared with the assistant's Google account.
  • Why: to answer the owner's questions about his schedule and include his events in his daily summary.
  • Where data goes: events are read on demand by the owner's private server and shown to the owner. They may be summarised by the AI model the owner uses. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Storage: events are not stored, except what the owner asks the assistant to keep. The access token is stored encrypted at rest in backups and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: arrecia.agent@gmail.com

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
